Frameworks

Four frameworks, one set of controls

Most of what SOC 2 asks for, ISO 27001 asks for too. Vellum maps each control once and counts it everywhere it applies — so the second certificate costs a fraction of the first.

SOC 2Type I & Type II An independent auditor's report on how you handle customer data — the one US enterprise buyers usually ask for. Type I checks the design of your controls on one date; Type II checks they actually ran, over 3–12 months.
ISO 270012022 revision An international certificate for running an information-security management system. Recognised worldwide — European and multinational buyers often ask for this instead of SOC 2.
GDPREU data protection EU law on personal data, not a certificate — nobody can certify you. You show compliance through records of processing, contracts with vendors and a process for handling data requests.
HIPAAUS health data US law for handling health information. It applies if you touch patient data on behalf of healthcare clients, and is evidenced through safeguards and signed agreements rather than an audit report.
